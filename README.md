# BG Sales Performance Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyses retail sales performance using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into sales across retail chains, stores, territories, and competitive markets to support informed business decisions.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

## Business Objectives
1. Clean and prepare the sales dataset.
2. Analyse total sales and annual sales performance.
3. Compare sales performance across retail chains and territories.
4. Identify the top-performing stores.
5. Evaluate sales efficiency based on selling area.
6. Investigate competitive and non-competitive store performance.
7. Provide actionable business recommendations.

## Key Performance Indicators
- **Total Stores:** 104
- **Total Sales:** Approximately $75M
- **Annual Sales:** Approximately $113M

## Key Findings
- Ohio recorded the highest total sales at approximately $16.47M.
- Fashions Direct generated the highest overall sales at approximately $48.02M.
- Fashions Direct operates 37 stores, compared with 67 Lindseys stores.
- The dataset contains 80 non-competitive and 24 competitive stores.

## Business Recommendations
1. Investigate the factors contributing to Ohio's strong sales performance.
2. Evaluate underperforming stores and territories for improvement opportunities.
3. Compare sales efficiency across retail chains rather than relying only on total revenue.
4. Study high-performing stores to identify practices that may be replicated elsewhere.
5. Consider operating costs, profitability, staffing, and customer demand before making expansion decisions.
